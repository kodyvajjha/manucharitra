The Story of Manu, with a word-by-word gloss and an English rendering

The Telugu text is Peddana's Svārociṣa-manusambhavamu. Cantos 1–4 were taken from a printed edition and corrected against the text on Telugu Wikisource. Cantos 5 and 6 follow the text published by Andhra Bharati, transcribed twice and the two copies compared, then scanned line by line against the metre; the few places where it has been corrected are marked in the notes. The pratipadārtham splits each verse after sandhi and glosses it phrase by phrase in the order of the Telugu; the rendering below it is freer.

Verses 5–7 of the first canto are Koundinya Vajjha's own translation. The rest is new work done for this edition.

Recite reads a verse aloud line by line with your device's own Telugu voice. It speaks the words plainly; it does not sing them to a rāgam the way padyams are recited.

Study, in the bar at the top, has two optional layers, both off until you turn them on; the page remembers your choice on this device. Metre marks scan every line by rule: U over a long syllable, | over a short one, a line over each gaṇa, the yati letters underlined and the prāsa letter in blue. A line whose marks are faint does not fit its metre as printed. The memory drill fades a verse in stages, to the first letter of each word, then of each line, then nothing, and with metre marks on, the marks stay behind as a skeleton.
